Before you send a drawing

Offshore worries, answered.

The five questions engineering managers ask before they send us a drawing.

Quality: who checks the drawings?

Every deliverable goes through multi-stage QA before issue: peer review, a senior check, verification against the code for your jurisdiction, and a check against your own drafting standards. Fixed-price projects include two revision rounds; on managed-service engagements, revisions come back within 24 hours.

Standards: will you draw to our codes and templates?

We work to the standard that applies to your project: NEC (US), IEC 60364 and IEC 61439, AS/NZS 3000 (Australia and New Zealand), CSA C22.1 (Canada) and BS 7671 (UK). We draw inside your title blocks, layer conventions, cell and block libraries, not ours.

IP and NDA: what happens to our files?

A mutual NDA is signed before any source material is shared, and every engineer is bound by an individual confidentiality agreement. Project access is segregated per client and revoked when the engagement ends. If you prefer, we work inside your Citrix, VDI or client-managed VPN, so your drawings never leave your environment.

Stamps: who signs the drawings?

Where a stamp is required, PE-stamped drawings are available in applicable US jurisdictions through our partnered Professional Engineers. For Australian and UK projects we partner with Chartered Engineers. Tell us the stamp requirement at scoping and we will confirm coverage before work starts.

Time zones: when can we talk to the team?

Production runs from our Pakistan office, 09:00–18:00 Pakistan Standard Time (PKT, UTC+5). Pakistan does not use daylight saving.

FAQ

Questions electrical managers ask us

Which electrical codes do your engineers work to?
NEC 2023 (US), IEC 60364 and IEC 61439 (international), AS/NZS 3000 (Australia/NZ), CSA C22.1 (Canada), and BS 7671 (UK). Every deliverable is QA'd against the applicable jurisdiction standard as part of our standard process.
Can you work inside our AutoCAD or Microstation templates?
Yes. We match your title blocks, cell libraries, layer naming, pen tables, and drawing conventions exactly. We can also work inside your hosted environment via Citrix, VDI, or a client-managed VPN.
What is your typical turnaround on a single-line diagram?
3–5 business days for first issue on standard SLDs. Complex MCC or switchgear packages run 2–3 weeks. Revisions under our managed-service engagements are returned within 24 hours.
Do you perform arc flash and coordination studies?
Yes. We perform IEEE 1584-compliant arc flash studies, protection coordination, and short-circuit analysis using ETAP and SKM Power Tools. Reports are delivered with incident energy labels ready for field printing.
How do you handle revisions and change orders?
Project-based engagements include two revision rounds in the fixed price. Managed-service clients receive unlimited revisions with a 24-hour SLA. Any additional change orders are quoted in writing before work begins — no surprise invoices.
