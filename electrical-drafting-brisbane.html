§01 — Why Brisbane

Queensland runs on drafting capacity that flexes with project cycles.

Queensland is home to the Bowen and Surat Basin coal operations, CSG production, LNG export terminals, and a dense Brisbane-based engineering consultancy sector. Every one of these depends on electrical documentation that can scale up when a project is in front of it and scale down when the cycle passes.

We act as the scalable drafting desk for Brisbane-based consultancies and QLD operators. We check every drawing against AS/NZS 3000 (wiring rules), AS/NZS 3008.1.1 (cable selection), the AS/NZS 61439 series (switchboard and MCC assemblies) and the AS/NZS 60079 series (explosive atmospheres), inside your Microstation or AutoCAD Electrical templates, under your title block.

Quality: who checks the drawings?

Every deliverable goes through multi-stage QA before issue: peer review, a senior check, verification against the code for your jurisdiction, and a check against your own drafting standards. Revision allowances and response windows are agreed as part of the project scope.

Standards: will you draw to our codes and templates?

We work to the standard that applies to your project: NEC (US), IEC 60364 and IEC 61439, AS/NZS 3000 (Australia and New Zealand), CSA C22.1 (Canada) and BS 7671 (UK). We draw inside your title blocks, layer conventions, cell and block libraries, not ours.

IP and NDA: what happens to our files?

A mutual NDA is signed before any source material is shared, and every engineer is bound by an individual confidentiality agreement. Project access is segregated per client and revoked when the engagement ends. If you prefer, we work inside your Citrix, VDI or client-managed VPN, so your drawings never leave your environment.

Stamps: who signs the drawings?

If your package requires a PE, Chartered Engineer or other statutory sign-off, tell us during scoping. We agree the responsible party and review requirements before production starts.

Time zones: when can we talk to the team?

Our production team is based in Pakistan (PKT, UTC+5). We agree your project’s working hours and handover arrangements during scoping. The table below uses a 09:00–18:00 PKT reference window to help plan the overlap; it is not a committed project schedule.

§03 — Brisbane-Specific FAQ

Questions QLD engineering managers ask us

Do you work with Queensland coal and CSG operators?
Yes. Queensland coal (Bowen Basin, Surat Basin), CSG operations, and downstream gas processing. AS/NZS 60079 hazardous-area specialists for mining and gas applications.
What is your working-hours overlap with AEST?
Our working day is 09:00–18:00 PKT (UTC+5), which is 14:00–23:00 AEST (UTC+10). That gives three shared working hours, 14:00–17:00 AEST, all year because Queensland does not use daylight saving. Mark-ups sent in the Brisbane morning are in production the same afternoon.
Can you work to Queensland-specific mining standards?
Yes. Documentation for sites under the Coal Mining Safety and Health Act 1999 and Coal Mining Safety and Health Regulation 2017, or the Mining and Quarrying Safety and Health Act 1999, plus operator-specific standards for major QLD coal and gas producers.
Do you subcontract through Brisbane engineering consultancies?
Yes. Most QLD engagements run as subcontractor arrangements — we act as production drafting inside your title block, invisible to your end client. Standard flow-down NDAs and IP clauses apply.
