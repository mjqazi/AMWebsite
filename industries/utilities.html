§03 — Utilities FAQ

Questions utility engineering managers ask us

Do you work on transmission as well as distribution?
Yes. Transmission substation documentation (up to 500 kV), distribution network design (up to 33 kV), and transmission/distribution interface stations. Protection coordination for both.
Can you integrate with our GIS system?
Yes. SkyCAD is our primary platform for GIS-integrated utility documentation. We also work with ESRI, Autodesk Utility, and client-specific GIS environments.
What protection-study capability do you have?
IEEE 1584 arc flash, ANSI/IEC protection coordination, short-circuit studies, and load-flow analysis in ETAP or SKM Power Tools. Reports formatted for utility regulator submission.
Do you have NERC CIP / security-aware workflows?
Yes. We work inside client-hosted environments with isolated access, no data egress, and audit-logged activity for NERC CIP-aware workflows. Individual engineer NDAs and background checks on senior staff.
