PV drawing packages within an EPC programme.

For an industrial or utility PV package, agree the equipment inputs, connection interfaces and review responsibilities before drawing production.

Start at the plant interface.

Supply the equipment information, current SLD, site layout and proposed connection point. The drawing scope can include the PV arrangement, electrical single-line, equipment references and the connection documentation agreed with your engineering team.

Identify the utility or reviewer requirements in the brief. Submission, design calculations, certification and any field verification need named owners rather than being assumed from the drawing scope.

One package, several inputs.

  • PV and inverter equipment information
  • Existing supply and distribution references
  • Plant load and connection information
  • Drawing and equipment naming conventions
  • Review comments and issue requirements
  • Native files, PDFs and the drawing register at handover
