§01 — Overview

Commercial construction doesn't forgive slow drawings.

High-rise risers, healthcare redundancy, data center distribution — commercial electrical documentation is coordination-intensive and rework-expensive. Every clash that survives to construction costs weeks and six-figure change orders. For full mechanical, electrical and plumbing production, see MEP drafting outsourcing.

We produce coordinated BIM-integrated commercial electrical documentation with clash detection built into the delivery workflow. Your MEP consultancy gets scalable production support; your project gets coordinated construction sets.

§03 — Commercial FAQ

Questions MEP consultancy managers ask us

What commercial building types do you handle?
Commercial high-rise, healthcare, data centers, educational, institutional, hospitality, and mixed-use. Each has specific code overlays and client standards we maintain libraries for.
Do you handle data center electrical documentation?
Yes. Data center MEP, UPS distribution, redundancy documentation (N+1, 2N), and commissioning packages. We work inside client BIM environments including BIM 360 and ProjectWise.
Can you do coordination with architects and structural?
Yes. Revit collaborative workflows with architectural, structural, and mechanical teams. Navisworks clash detection integrated into deliverables.
Do you work on healthcare projects with NEC Art. 517?
Yes. NEC Art. 517 healthcare compliance including essential electrical system (EES), isolated power systems, and room-category documentation per facility type.
