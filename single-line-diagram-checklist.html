Single-line diagram checklist.

49 checks to run on a single-line diagram before it is issued: document control, supply, transformers, switchboards, protection, cables, loads, alternate supplies, earthing and cross-checks.

New to SLDs? Start with what a single-line diagram is and what it shows. The checklist is written for both Australian / New Zealand projects (AS/NZS 3000) and US projects (NEC); items that only apply in one market say so.

Project
Drawing no. / rev
Checked by
Date

1. Title block and document control

2. Supply and point of connection

3. Transformers

4. Switchboards, switchgear and MCCs

5. Protective devices

6. Cables and conductors

7. Loads, motors and drives

8. Alternate supplies: generators, UPS, PV and batteries

9. Earthing, bonding and arc flash

10. Final cross-checks before issue

This checklist is a review aid, not engineering advice. It does not replace the applicable standards, the network operator’s or utility’s requirements, or review by a competent engineer.
