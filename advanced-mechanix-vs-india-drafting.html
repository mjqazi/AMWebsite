Choosing a team for an industrial drawing package.

A useful vendor comparison starts with the work, not the country on the proposal. Ask how the team will handle your inputs, standards, review comments and source files.

Compare the review process, not just the rate.

A large production team can be right for a repeatable drawing workload. A smaller specialist team can suit a package with changing engineering inputs and frequent technical queries. Neither model is automatically better; the fit depends on who owns the design basis and how much review the package needs.

Before comparing prices, give each bidder the same drawing register, example source files, drafting standard and review allowance. Otherwise you are comparing different jobs.

Questions worth putting in the brief.

  • Who reviews equipment tags and references across the full package?
  • How are missing inputs and conflicting mark-ups raised?
  • Which templates, libraries and software versions will be used?
  • What is included in the review and revision allowance?
  • Who owns design decisions, calculations and statutory sign-off?
  • What source files and issue information arrive at handover?

A defined sample package can help establish the workflow. Agree the scope, price and acceptance criteria first. A free offer alone does not tell you how the team will handle the rest of the project.
